Private & Custom Tours

A Cape Town Trip Designed Around You

Not every traveler wants a fixed departure time or a set group itinerary. Our private touring service exists for exactly that reason — a dedicated guide, a private vehicle, and a plan shaped entirely by what you want to see and how much time you have to see it. Whether it's a single afternoon or a multi-day journey through the Western Cape, the day belongs to you.

There's no set menu here. We combine the routes and highlights from our signature tours — Cape Point, the Winelands, Table Mountain, the Garden Route, and beyond — or build something entirely new around a special occasion, a tight schedule, or a specific interest. You tell us what matters, and we handle the logistics, the pacing, and the local know-how.
