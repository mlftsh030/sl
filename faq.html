Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your Cape Town tour with us.

Home FAQ
How do I book a tour with SL Tours?

You can book directly through WhatsApp at +27 67 012 7776, by filling in the enquiry form on any tour page or our Contact page, or by calling us. We'll confirm availability, pickup details, and next steps — usually within the same day.

Where will I be picked up from?

We offer pick-up and drop-off from your Cape Town accommodation for all standard tours. Once you book, we'll confirm the exact pickup time and location with you directly.

Are your tour prices per person?

Yes, all listed tour prices are per person unless stated otherwise — for example, Airport Transfers are priced per trip, not per person. Private and custom tours are quoted individually based on your itinerary and group size.

Is there a minimum number of guests for a tour?

Most of our fixed-departure tours require a minimum of 5 guests to run. If your group is smaller, we can often still arrange your tour as a private booking — get in touch and we'll confirm availability and pricing.

What is included in the tour price?

Every tour page lists exactly what is included and excluded. In general, pick-up and drop-off, a qualified guide, an air-conditioned vehicle, and bottled water are included across our tours, with entrance fees, meals, and tastings included on specific packages — see the "Included / Not Included" section on each tour page for full detail.

Can I book a private or custom tour?

Yes. Our Private & Custom Tours option lets you build a flexible itinerary around your schedule, interests, and group size, with a dedicated guide and private vehicle. Rates are quoted on request based on your plans.

Is SL Tours safe and reliable?

Safety and punctuality are central to how we operate. All tours are run in well-maintained, air-conditioned vehicles by qualified, professional guides, with a strong track record of on-time pickups and attentive service. Read more on our Testimonials page.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Plans change — message us on WhatsApp or by phone as soon as you know, and we'll do our best to reschedule your tour to a date that works for you.

Do you offer airport transfers as a standalone service?

Yes — our Airport Transfer service is available as a standalone booking between Cape Town International Airport and your accommodation, priced per trip, with no obligation to book any other tour.
