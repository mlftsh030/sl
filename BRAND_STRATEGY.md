## PHASE 1 — Brand Strategy

### Positioning statement
> **SL Tours is Cape Town's premium local experience company — offering authentic, safe, unforgettable, and professionally curated South African tours.**

### Differentiators to lead with
1. **Locally owned, locally guided** — proudly Black-owned Cape Town business, founded and run by a qualified professional tourist guide (Lisolethu Nkcaza), not a franchise or reseller.
2. **Personal, not mass-market** — small-group and private options, tailored itineraries, direct WhatsApp access to a real person.
3. **Safety and reliability as a promise, not a slogan** — well-maintained air-conditioned vehicles, punctual pickups, qualified guides.
4. **Breadth without dilution** — city culture, wine country, wildlife safari, coastline, and township heritage tours all run by the same trusted team.

### Visual identity
- **Palette (official SL Tours brand colors):** Primary Blue `#1F4AA8` and Secondary Blue `#2D63D3` (trust, safety, professionalism), Primary Orange `#F26A2E` and Accent Orange `#FF8A4C` (warmth, energy, African hospitality), Midnight Navy `#14213D` (headings/footer), Soft Ivory `#FAFAF7` (backgrounds), White `#FFFFFF`.
- **Typography:** Headings — Poppins (H1 64px/700, H2 44px/700, H3 28px/600); Body — Inter (18px/1.7, small 15px/1.6); Buttons — Poppins SemiBold, 16px, 0.02em letter-spacing.
- **Shape language:** pill-radius buttons (999px), 20px rounded cards with a 4px orange top-border accent on featured tours, soft elevation shadows, generous white space, sticky navbar with subtle blur.
- **Hero treatment:** cinematic Cape Town photography under a fixed navy-to-blue diagonal gradient overlay (`linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(20,33,61,0.82), rgba(31,74,168,0.68))`).
- **Imagery:** Real SL Tours photography only (Cape Point, city tour, Winelands, Township, Garden Route, Aquila safari, West Coast, owner portrait) — no unrelated stock substitutions for hero/tour content.

### Tone of voice
Confident, warm, human. Every page speaks like a knowledgeable local host, not a booking engine. Sentences are short. Copy sells the *feeling* of the experience (the wind on Chapman's Peak, the first sip of Stellenbosch wine) before it sells the logistics.

### Target audience
- **Primary:** International leisure travelers (UK, US, EU, Australia) aged 28–65, first-time or repeat visitors to Cape Town, booking 1–4 activities during a 3–10 day stay, comfortable paying a premium for safety, reliability, and a knowledgeable guide over the cheapest listing.
- **Secondary:** Domestic South African travelers and business travelers with layover/free days wanting a trusted half-day or airport-transfer option.
- **Tertiary:** Groups/families booking private, custom, multi-day itineraries (Garden Route, custom combinations).

Global elements on every page: glass sticky navbar, floating WhatsApp button (bottom-right, all breakpoints), footer with contact block + nav + socials.

### User journey / conversion funnel
1. **Land** on Home via organic search ("Cape Town tours"), Google Ads, or social referral.
2. **Orient** via hero + trust strip (licensed guides, safe transport, local expertise) — answers "can I trust this operator?" in 5 seconds.
3. **Browse** Featured Experiences cards on Home → click through to Tours grid or directly to a tour detail page.
4. **Evaluate** on the tour detail page: quick facts, itinerary, inclusions/exclusions, price, real photos, social proof.
5. **Convert** via one of two paths:
   - Fast path: WhatsApp CTA (pre-filled message) — zero-friction for mobile users, matches existing successful pattern.
   - Formal path: inquiry/booking form on Contact page or tour page → routes to WhatsApp/email.
6. **Reassure** — Testimonials and FAQ pages exist to remove last-mile doubt for hesitant visitors before they commit.
7. **Repeat/refer** — Private Tours page captures higher-value custom/group bookings that don't fit fixed packages.

Every template keeps the WhatsApp button and a "Book This Tour" CTA above the fold or within one scroll — no page should be more than one tap from a conversion action.

### Technical SEO implemented in code
- Semantic HTML5 (`<header>`, `<nav>`, `<main>`, `<section>`, `<article>`, `<footer>`) with a single `<h1>` per page.
- Descriptive `alt` text on every image (real tour names/locations, not "image.jpg").
- Internal linking: every tour card links to its own detail page; every tour detail page links back to /tours.html and to Contact.
- `LocalBusiness`/`TravelAgency` JSON-LD schema on Home and Contact (name, address, phone, geo, sameAs social links, priceRange).
- `FAQPage` JSON-LD schema on the FAQ page.
- `Product`/`TouristTrip` schema opportunity noted per tour page (price, duration).
- Canonical tags, Open Graph + Twitter Card meta on every page.
- Lazy-loaded below-the-fold images (`loading="lazy"`), deferred non-critical JS, minimal render-blocking CSS.

## PHASE 6 — UI/UX Specifications

- **Colors:** see palette above; implemented as CSS custom properties in `assets/css/style.css` (`--sl-blue`, `--sl-blue-secondary`, `--sl-orange`, `--sl-orange-accent`, `--sl-navy`, `--sl-ivory`) and mapped onto the template's existing `--e-global-color-*` variables so every component inherits the new palette without a rebuild. Earlier `--sl-ocean`/`--sl-teal`/`--sl-gold`/`--sl-sand` names are kept as legacy aliases pointing at the new values, so nothing else in the codebase needed to change.
- **Typography:** Poppins (headings/buttons) via Google Fonts, Inter (body) via Google Fonts — swapped in at the stylesheet's single `@import` line.
- **Components:** rounded 20px tour cards, soft-shadow elevation on hover, glass/blurred sticky navbar, floating WhatsApp action button (all pages), animated stat counters (guests hosted, tours run, years combined guiding experience, average rating), testimonial carousel, lightbox gallery, validated inquiry form.
- **Spacing:** template's existing section rhythm retained (large vertical section padding, consistent container gutters) — no new spacing system needed since Atreves already matches the "spacious, elegant" brief.
- **Animation:** AOS (Animate On Scroll) fade-up/zoom-in on section entry (already wired in template), smooth-scroll enabled, hover translate/scale on buttons and cards — all subtle, no motion that delays interaction.
