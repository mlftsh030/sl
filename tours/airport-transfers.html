The Experience

One Less Thing To Worry About

After a long-haul flight, the last thing you want is uncertainty about how you're getting to your accommodation. SL Tours' airport transfer service is built around reliability — a professional driver in a clean, air-conditioned vehicle, tracking your flight in real time, waiting for you with a meet & greet at arrivals whether you land early, on schedule, or delayed.

There's no scrambling for a taxi rank, no language barrier to navigate, and no guessing whether the driver knows where your guesthouse or hotel actually is. Every transfer runs point-to-point between Cape Town International Airport and your accommodation, with your comfort and your schedule as the only priorities. It's the same reliability whether you're arriving for the first time or heading back to the airport for a flight home.

Because it's private and on request, you set the pickup time — early morning departures and late-night arrivals are handled the same way, with the same attention. It's a small detail that makes a real difference to how a trip begins and ends.

How It Works

Simple, Reliable, Stress-Free

Step 1

Flight Monitoring. Once your transfer is booked, we track your flight number so your pickup time automatically adjusts to early arrivals or delays.

Step 2

Meet & Greet at Arrivals. Your driver waits for you inside the terminal at Cape Town International Airport, ready to help with luggage.

Step 3

Direct Transfer to Your Accommodation. A comfortable, air-conditioned drive straight to your hotel or guesthouse — no detours, no shared stops.
