The Experience

Slow Travel, Big Scenery

The Garden Route is the drive South Africans dream about, and this three-day journey gives you time to actually feel it rather than rush past it. You'll leave Cape Town and travel east into a landscape that changes by the hour — indigenous forest, dramatic cliffs, quiet lagoons — with stops built around the towns that make the route famous. Two nights of shared accommodation mean you're never watching the clock, and two dinners plus two breakfasts are included so the trip runs smoothly from morning coffee to evening plans.

Knysna anchors the journey with its lagoon and forested Heads, while Tsitsikamma delivers some of the most dramatic coastal scenery in the country — ancient forest canopy walks, the suspension bridge over the Storms River mouth, and cliffs that drop straight into the Indian Ocean. A guided kayaking outing is included, giving you a different vantage point on the water most visitors only ever see from the road. Wilderness adds its own quieter beauty, with lakes and long open beaches, before the route continues toward Mossel Bay and the game-rich plains of Botlierskop.

This is a tour for travelers who want more than a checklist — who'd rather spend three unhurried days getting to know a coastline than see it in a single long day. Optional extras like bungee jumping at Tsitsikamma can be arranged on request, and your guide handles the logistics so you can simply enjoy the drive.
