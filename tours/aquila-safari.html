The Experience

Africa's Big Five, Without The Long Haul

Most travelers assume a genuine Big Five safari means flying deep into the bush for days. Aquila Private Game Reserve proves otherwise. Around two hours from Cape Town, on the way to the Karoo, this malaria-free reserve puts lion, elephant, rhino, buffalo and leopard within reach of a single, comfortable day trip — return transport included, so you're back at your accommodation by evening.

The day is built around a guided game drive in an open safari vehicle with a professional ranger who reads the landscape and knows where the animals are likely to be. Between drives there's a welcome drink on arrival and a buffet lunch overlooking the reserve, with free time afterward to relax at the lodge before the drive back to Cape Town. It's unhurried, well organized, and genuinely exciting — the kind of day that turns "we saw a safari brochure" into "we saw a lion."

Because Aquila is malaria-free, it's an easy yes for families and travelers who'd rather not manage anti-malarial medication on a short trip. Combined with the convenience of a same-day return from Cape Town, it's one of the most popular add-ons for visitors who want the safari experience without rearranging their whole itinerary.
