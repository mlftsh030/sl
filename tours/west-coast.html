The Experience

A Different Side Of The Cape

Where the Peninsula tours show off dramatic cliffs and mountain passes, the West Coast reveals a quieter, wide-open South Africa — flat horizons, fynbos-covered dunes, and skies that seem to stretch forever. This full-day tour heads north out of Cape Town to !Khwa ttu, a nature reserve built around San heritage and culture, where a guided visit walks you through the history, traditions and knowledge of the region's first inhabitants against a backdrop of rolling coastal scrubland.

From there the day continues into West Coast National Park, home to the Langebaan Lagoon and, depending on season, some of the country's most spectacular wildflower displays. Entrance fees are covered for both stops, along with any additional entrance fees along the route, so there's nothing to sort out on the day — just time to walk the trails, watch the birdlife on the lagoon, and take in a stretch of coastline most visitors never see.

Lunch is included, giving the day a relaxed, unhurried pace, and your guide handles the driving in an air-conditioned vehicle so you can spend the trip looking out the window instead of at a map. It's an excellent choice for travelers who've already done the classic Cape Point run and want to see a genuinely different landscape.
